Our Services

Powering progress through every phase of infrastructure development

Saudi Delta Company provides high-impact electrical solutions that support growth, resilience, and the future of energy across the Kingdom.

Distribution Networks

End-to-end delivery of reliable MV and LV distribution networks up to 34 kV, from planning and installation to integration with existing infrastructure.

Overhead Networks

Construction and upgrading of overhead distribution lines up to 34 kV, including poles, conductors, and associated equipment.

Underground Networks

Installation of MV/LV underground cable systems, including trenching/HDD, duct banks, cable pulling, joints & terminations, protection, and earthing.

Maintenance Services

Planned and corrective maintenance services aimed at extending asset life and minimizing operational disruptions.

Road & Asphalt Works

Execution of roadworks and asphalt paving associated with power infrastructure projects, ensuring durability, safety, and compliance with municipal standards.

Future Developments

Strategic expansion into advanced power solutions and emerging infrastructure technologies aligned with Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030.
